Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

BookMySofa is operated by T73 Furniture, Ernakulam, Kerala, India. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

BookMySofa does not require customers to create an account or provide any personal information. Customers can browse all furniture products without registering or logging in. We do not collect, store, or process any personal data from customers browsing the app.

2. Information Collected from the Shop Owner

The shop owner (administrator) logs in using an email address and password. We store:

  • Email address
  • Hashed password (never stored in plain text)
  • Account creation timestamp

This information is used solely to authenticate the shop owner and manage the product catalog.

3. Product Images

Product images uploaded by the shop owner are stored on Cloudinary, a third-party cloud storage service. These images are publicly accessible through URLs embedded in the product listings. Cloudinary's privacy policy applies to image storage.

4. WhatsApp Enquiries

When a customer taps "Chat on WhatsApp", the app opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. This action is handled entirely by WhatsApp. We do not intercept, store, or process any messages or phone numbers from this interaction. WhatsApp's own privacy policy governs that communication.

5. Favourites

If a customer saves products to Favourites, this data is stored locally on their device only, using device-level secure storage. It is never transmitted to our servers.

6. Cookies and Tracking

BookMySofa does not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, or any third-party tracking tools. We do not track your behaviour within the app.

7. Data Security

All communication between the app and our server uses HTTPS encryption. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and are never stored or transmitted in plain text. We do not share any data with third parties for marketing purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

BookMySofa is a furniture catalog application suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
